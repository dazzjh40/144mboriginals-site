Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 May 2026

1.44MB Originals respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information may be collected through 144mboriginals.com and how it is used.

Who we are

1.44MB Originals operates 144mboriginals.com. For privacy questions, contact hello@144mboriginals.com.

Information we collect

When you browse the site, we may collect optional analytics data if you accept analytics cookies. If you email a bespoke request, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, product idea, sizes, quantities and deadline.

How we use information

We use enquiry information to respond to you, discuss bespoke products and keep a record of the request. We use optional analytics data to understand which pages are visited and improve the website.

Lawful bases

For analytics, we rely on your consent. For bespoke enquiries, we use your information to take steps at your request before a possible order and for our legitimate interest in responding to customer enquiries. We may also keep limited records where needed for legal or accounting reasons.

Services we use

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Purchases currently continue through Etsy, and fulfilment may involve print-on-demand partners such as Printify. Google Analytics only loads if you accept analytics cookies. These services may process data under their own terms and privacy notices.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry emails only as long as needed to handle the request, manage follow-up questions, maintain business records or meet legal obligations. Analytics information is controlled through Google Analytics retention settings.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent for analytics cookies. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how your data is handled.
