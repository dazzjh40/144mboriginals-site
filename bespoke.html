How it works

Simple, practical, no mystery checkout.

1

Send the idea, product type, size range, quantity and deadline.

2

3

If it is a fit, the final product can be created for ordering through the most suitable route.

Include this in your email

  • Product: tee, hoodie, mug, notebook, tote or other gift.
  • Wording, name, date, year, hobby or theme.
  • Preferred colours and any style references.
  • Sizes, quantity and deadline.
  • Whether you need a one-off gift or repeat ordering.
